# Paid open-world campaign reference — 22 October 2026

Historical PR8 reference: the current price curve and comparison are documented in [Opening prices](2026-10-22-opening-prices.md).

The final modern catalog completes a fresh, fully paid rocket campaign in **3h 55m 15.5s** on seed 42 and **3h 55m 2.75s** on seed 43, using Godot 4.5 and the real 0.25-second simulation. Both final city snapshots pass save validation.

These are **zero-delay sequential operating references**, not measured human averages or globally optimal times. Planning and command entry consume no simulated time. The seed-42 policy executes 7,112 commands, including 239 route builds and 5,021 individual belt demolitions; automated route housekeeping would take additional time through the touch UI. Ongoing parallel factories, more income upgrades, different investments, or less frequent management can change completion time. Two seeds establish reproducibility for this policy, not a population estimate of player completion time.

## Operating policy and accounting

- Start with `fresh(0, true, seed)` and its empty map and 2,250 cash. Build the pioneer forest through `command`; buy the two early upgrades, linked sawmill, and linked warehouse using earned money.
- Keep the original forestry line operating. Build real mines on scattered nodes and collect their outputs in adjacent warehouses. Raw production continues while other work runs.
- Use one processor per product, plus two generators with dedicated coal mines. Buy processor input capacity before collecting ingredients. Start with batches of at most 20 and increase to at most 40 after the space stage when funding allows.
- For each ingredient, use `World.route_quote` and a paid `buildRoute`, dispatch from the actual source warehouse, wait for physical arrival in the processor, then disconnect before routing the next ingredient. Reserved, paused departure ports prevent residual material from leaking into another line. Demolished belts recover their contents through the normal game rule; there is no direct inventory transfer.
- Fund construction and upgrades by producing and selling the highest reached cash-scale product. After new cash scales, buy upstream production upgrades whose individual prices are at most half one unit of that product's base sale value.
- Disable automatic selling while accumulating construction or rocket stock, use zero reserves for dispatch, and physically route each of the six rocket materials into the launchpad before issuing `launch`.

There is no cash, resource, entity, unlock, stage, clock, or power injection. Commands, ticking, production, routing, sales, refunds, construction materials, research, capacity upgrades, and rocket delivery use the actual game code. No offline production, orders, projects, or artificial reaction delays are used.

Cash spent is actual command debits; it excludes the sale opportunity cost of materials. Refunds are tracked separately. Each checkpoint satisfies `2,250 + earned + refunds − spent = cash`.

## Verification and reproduction

Both independent fresh replays passed: seed42 **7,112commands /235.258333minutes**, seed43 **7,028commands /235.045833minutes**. Replays matched cash paid, refunds, ending cash, total earned, all produced/sold resources and all rocket deliveries, then passed save validation. The seed42 replay is included in CI.

Catalog SHA-256: `b76ce4e70fac40ba2ec45eb9aa444fd8a207e033591b919caebf12973c40a9ba`.

Run from the project root with the pinned Godot 4.5 binary:

```sh
export XDG_DATA_HOME=/tmp/rust-pixel/data
export XDG_CONFIG_HOME=/tmp/rust-pixel/config
export XDG_CACHE_HOME=/tmp/rust-pixel/cache
.runtime/godot/Godot_v4.5-stable_linux.x86_64 --headless --path . --script tools/campaign_balance.gd
.runtime/godot/Godot_v4.5-stable_linux.x86_64 --headless --path . --script tools/campaign_balance.gd -- --seed=43
.runtime/godot/Godot_v4.5-stable_linux.x86_64 --headless --path . --script tools/campaign_balance.gd -- --opening-baseline
.runtime/godot/Godot_v4.5-stable_linux.x86_64 --headless --path . --script tools/campaign_balance.gd -- --replay=docs/balance/2026-10-22-campaign-zero-latency.json
.runtime/godot/Godot_v4.5-stable_linux.x86_64 --headless --path . --script tools/campaign_balance.gd -- --replay=docs/balance/2026-10-22-campaign-zero-latency-seed43.json
```

The simulator emits progress every ten simulated minutes and stops if a requested product makes no progress for 300 seconds. Partial files have `launched: 0` and are rejected as replay completion evidence. `--review-seconds=N` is an optional polling-policy experiment; no such delay is used in the reported runs.

The obsolete 21 October campaign began with a different starter layout and economy. It is preserved separately and is not used as evidence for this empty-start balance. The preliminary doubled-cycle policy took 356.2 minutes, and the 1.25× later-cycle policy took 252.77–253.30 minutes; the final 1.10× adjustment reduces actual production time rather than adding or removing elapsed-time padding.
